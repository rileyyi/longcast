      sources

      gfs / NOAA

      US global forecast model. The ensemble runs many versions to show a range of plausible outcomes.

      ecmwf

      European global model, often useful for medium-range patterns. Ensemble spread helps indicate uncertainty.

      icon / DWD

      German global model. An independent forecast signal to compare against the US and European systems.

      gem / ECCC

      Canadian global model. Adds another independent view of temperature and precipitation trends.

      ensemble spread

      Wider member-to-member spread usually means lower confidence. Agreement is a signal, not a guarantee.

      climate baseline

      30-year averages provide seasonal context; they are not a prediction for any one day.

      lead time

      Daily detail is most useful early in the outlook. Later weeks are better read as broad tendencies.

      adjustments

      Long-range ensembles are aligned to the higher-resolution first-week outlook to reduce early bias.
