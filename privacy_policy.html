Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Longcast ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Longcast is designed to operate without harvesting or storing your personal details. We do not collect, capture, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or precise location data.

2. Data Usage and Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics tools, tracking cookies, or advertising SDKs inside the application. No usage statistics or behavioral data are gathered or transmitted to external servers.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests standard network permissions strictly to enable WebGL and web content rendering features. No sensitive device features—such as your camera, microphone, contacts, or storage—are accessed without explicit permission.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not link to third-party services that collect information about you.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, children can safely use Longcast without data exposure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at your support email address.
